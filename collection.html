Our Story

The Story of Tulip Touch Café

Where Parisian Elegance Meets Artisanal Coffee Mastery

✦
Chapter I

The Vision & Heritage

The Tulip Touch Café was conceived as a dreamy sanctuary where classical European aesthetics meet warm contemporary hospitality. Inspired by romantic Parisian coffee houses and hand-crafted 2D comic art, every detail in our café is created to evoke wonder, warmth, and timeless beauty.

Chapter II

The Artisanal Craft

From single-origin hand-poured roasts to masterfully baked signature pastries, every recipe is crafted with genuine passion. Our baristas and chefs infuse every creation with culinary precision, ensuring that each cup served is a celebration of flavor and artistry.

Chapter III

The Café Ambiance

Surrounded by ornate baroque wall frames, warm dining tables, and soft golden light, Tulip Touch Café offers more than just exceptional food and drink—it is a cozy retreat designed for quiet indulgence, inspiring conversations, and cherished memories.
